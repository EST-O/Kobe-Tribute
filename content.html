Kobe Bryant

The Legacy

A Life Lived Without Limits

01

The Mamba Mentality

Kobe's legendary work ethic defined an era. He was known for arriving before dawn and staying long after teammates left, constantly pushing himself to find any edge he could gain over his opponents.

02

Five Championships

From the three-peat with Shaquille O'Neal (2000–2002) to back-to-back titles as the undisputed leader of the Lakers (2009–2010), Kobe proved he could win both ways — as a co-star and as the alpha.

03

Olympic Gold

Kobe brought his championship hunger to the international stage, winning gold medals with Team USA in the 2008 Beijing and 2012 London Olympics, cementing himself as a global icon of the sport.

04

Beyond Basketball

After retiring, Kobe earned an Academy Award for his animated short film "Dear Basketball." He was devoted to his family and to inspiring the next generation through his Mamba Sports Academy.

Career Timeline

1996

Drafted 13th Overall

Selected by the Charlotte Hornets and immediately traded to the Los Angeles Lakers at just 17 years old.

1998

First NBA All-Star

Became the youngest All-Star in NBA history at age 19, captivating fans with his electrifying dunks and skills.

2000

First Championship

Won the first of three consecutive NBA titles alongside Shaquille O'Neal and head coach Phil Jackson.

2006

81 Points

Scored 81 points against the Toronto Raptors — the second-highest single-game total in NBA history.

2008

MVP Season

Won the league MVP award and led Team USA to Olympic gold in Beijing, silencing any doubters about his legacy.

2016

Farewell for the Ages

Scored 60 points in his final game to close one of the most remarkable 20-year careers in sports history.
